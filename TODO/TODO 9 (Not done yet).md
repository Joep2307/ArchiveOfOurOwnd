# Automatically track new AO3 reads and infer completion from reading time

Automatically add a new fic to the library when the reader opens it on AO3.
Only add it to Finished after the reader has spent enough time reading it
to plausibly have finished it; opening or briefly clicking a fic is not
enough. Define the reading-time threshold during implementation, taking
fic length and the reader's reading speed into account.
